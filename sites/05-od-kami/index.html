03

How a quote works

Three steps, no cost.

  1. Tell Amber about your home

    Use the request form in section 05, or text, call or email her directly. Size, rooms and anything that needs extra attention.

  2. Amber follows up personally

    She talks through what your home needs and gives you a quote for it. There is no charge for the quote.

  3. You decide

    Ask any questions you have, including for references, before you choose.

  • Weekly, biweekly, one-time and deep cleaning are all quoted the same way.
  • Not sure which you need? Pick “Not sure yet” on the form.
